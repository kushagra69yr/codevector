Why Cursor Pagination?

🛡️
No Duplication: Since we page relative to a cursor timestamp rather than a row offset, new products added at the top do not shift the remaining catalog into your next page.
⚡
Constant O(1) Speed: Traditional offset-based queries (e.g. OFFSET 150000) require database engine scanning all preceding records. Cursors leverage indexes to jump directly to the target record.
